# ChickUp Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

ChickUp is a gentle break-reminder app. Your privacy is simple, because the app is simple.

**Data stored on your device only.** Your reminder schedule, settings, and check-in history are stored locally on your iPhone, in the app's own storage. They are never sent to us or to any server.

**Content downloads.** The app downloads animation and quote files from a public file repository hosted by GitHub, so new content can arrive without an app update. Those downloads are served by GitHub, which may process standard request information (such as your IP address) as described in GitHub's Privacy Statement. ChickUp itself does not collect, receive, or store any of that information.

**No accounts, no analytics, no ads.** ChickUp has no sign-up, contains no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, and does not track you across apps or websites. We do not collect, sell, or share personal data.

**Notifications** are scheduled and generated locally on your device.
